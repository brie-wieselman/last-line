Format

Here's what you get

A real example. Topic: "Why most budgeting apps fail by month two" · Talking head · 45 seconds. You get three like this, each with a different hook.

Contrarian94 words · ~45s

Cut it to four

0:00–0:04 · Hook stack

VERBALYou didn't quit budgeting. Budgeting quit you, somewhere around week six.
WRITTENWhy budgets die in month two
VISUALA phone with a budgeting app and a stack of unread notifications.

0:04–0:12 · The belief

Most people reach for more discipline and a stricter app. Then the app asks them for forty small decisions a week.

0:12–0:32 · The point

Your budget has too many categories. Every one is a decision you make at the checkout. Cut it to four: bills, food, fun, savings. Fewer choices means you actually open it.

0:32–0:40 · What to do

Tonight, merge every category into those four. Ten minutes, tops.

0:40–0:45 · Last line

A budget you open beats a perfect one you don't.

CTA

Follow for part two: the one setting that keeps it going.

Why this works
  • Contrarian hook: it flips the "you need discipline" belief in the first line.
  • One problem, one fix, one number (four categories) the viewer can act on tonight.

Voice

Make my voiceprint

Paste 4 to 6 real things you wrote, from different parts of your life: a work email or post, something personal, something where you argue a point, a casual text. Real writing only. Describing your style ("I'm warm and direct") does not count.

Your samples go to Gemini with your key to build the rules. They are not saved anywhere.

Already have one? Import it

A voiceprint is a set of rules pulled from your real writing. You can also make one with the free Voiceprint skill for Claude.
